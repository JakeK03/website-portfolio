KÜERBIS

(Work In Progress) Circadian Rythmn is an idea that began by trying to conjure the feeling of missing home. The rythmn I have is different in every place I live. The time I get up in New York means a three hour shift in time zones from back home in Portland. Seasons are different, and the sun is different. I wanted to be able to digitize that moment of glancing into an aspect of my life I normally can't see.

Through looking at a webcam of Mount Hood in Oregon—the backdrop to my childhood—I could feel a sense of connection.

By having the site function like an alarm, only working when the sun is up, allows me and the users to embrace limitations.
